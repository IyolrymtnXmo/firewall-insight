## Final prompts

### Earth and Moon

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: photorealistic cinematic background for an enterprise cybersecurity Space Technology landing page. Generate a wide 16:9 image, 2560x1440 if possible. Scene: deep near-black space (#020509), extremely subtle sparse stars. A huge scientifically plausible Earth occupies the RIGHT HALF of the frame, partially cropped past the right edge, with richly detailed oceans, continents, delicate clouds, night-side city lights and a very thin icy blue atmospheric rim. Earth from lunar orbit. A realistic charcoal grey lunar surface runs across the complete bottom 18% of the image in the foreground, craters and mineral texture, low horizon. LEFT HALF and upper left must remain dark clean negative space for HTML copy. Earth should occupy x=52% to 105%, y=0% to 85%, not centered. Restrained cool azure #4DA7E8 light; realistic photographic exposure, not bright fantasy. No spacecraft, no laptop, no people, no typography, no UI, no logos, no watermarks. Premium aerospace editorial realism.

Satellite placed slightly right of center; Earth horizon curved along the lower right with an icy blue atmospheric rim. Deep near-black negative space surrounding the subject, sparse barely visible stars. 3:2 wide composition with complete satellite visible, restrained #4DA7E8 rim lighting, cinematic high contrast, premium serious aerospace photography, physically plausible engineering and materials. Black corners blend naturally into #020509 website background. No text, labels, logos, watermark, people, neon fantasy, cartoons or illustration.
